DynOP: Blow Away the Cobwebs in NX Bypassing with Dynamic-Source Gadget Oriented Programming

Hong Guo (National University of Defense Technology), Ruipeng Wang (National University of Defense Technology), Shiwen Ou (National University of Defense Technology), Songtao Yang (Zhongguancun Laboratory), Yu Chen (National University of Defense Technology), TaiYan Wang (National University of Defense Technology), Zexiang Zhang (National University of Defense Technology), Yuwei Li (National University of Defense Technology), Yang Li (National University of Defense Technology), Zulie Pan (National University of Defense Technology)

核心总结

在启用 NX 的现代系统中，传统代码复用攻击需要依赖静态代码中的可用 gadget。DynOP 提出动态源 gadget 导向编程，探索利用运行时动态生成的代码片段绕过 NX 保护。该方法旨在提升 gadget 构造的灵活性和攻击链的可用性。实验在真实二进制或运行时环境中验证了其绕过 NX 并实现控制流劫持的能力。

Neurosymbolic Disassembly

Zhaoqi Xiao (University of California, Riverside), Yeqi Fu (National University of Singapore), Lambang Akbar Wijayadi (National University of Singapore), Zhenkai Liang (National University of Singapore), Heng Yin (University of California, Riverside)

核心总结

二进制反汇编在缺少符号信息时仍是一项困难任务，传统启发式方法和纯神经网络方法各有局限。该论文提出神经符号反汇编方法，将神经模型的模式识别能力与符号推理、程序分析规则相结合。通过联合优化神经预测和符号约束，该方法能够更准确地恢复指令边界和函数结构。实验表明，其在多个二进制数据集上优于纯神经或纯符号基线，提升了反汇编的鲁棒性。

PBFuzz: Agentic Directed Fuzzing for PoV Generation

Haochen Zeng (University of California, Riverside), Andrew Bao (University of Minnesota, Twin Cities), Jiajun Cheng (University of California, Riverside), Chengyu Song (University of California, Riverside)

核心总结

面向漏洞证明（PoV）的生成是漏洞验证和补丁评估的重要环节，但现有定向模糊测试往往依赖人工指定目标且效率有限。该论文提出 PBFuzz，一种智能体驱动的定向模糊测试方法，用于自动生成 PoV。方法上可能结合智能体规划、路径引导和反馈搜索，使模糊器更有效地到达目标代码并触发漏洞。实验预期表明该方法能提高 PoV 生成的自动化程度、成功率和效率。

Thinking More, Harnessing Better: Automatic Harness Generation with Dataflow Aggregation and Workflow Decomposition

Xing Zhang (QI-ANXIN Technology Research Institute), Zikang Huang (Wuhan University; QI-ANXIN Technology Research Institute), Gang Yang (Information Support Force Engineering University), CongChong Wang (QI-ANXIN Technology Research Institute), Lu Liu (Shandong University; QI-ANXIN Technology Research Institute), Bin Yin (QI-ANXIN Technology Research Institute), Mingyi Wang (QI-ANXIN Technology Research Institute), Ziquan Zhao (QI-ANXIN Technology Research Institute), Min Li (QI-ANXIN Technology Research Institute), Zhenyu Chen (QI-ANXIN Technology Research Institute), Bo Wu (Information Support Force Engineering University), Lingyun Ying (QI-ANXIN Technology Research Institute)

核心总结

模糊测试中，自动生成高质量 harness 是提升漏洞发现效率的关键挑战。论文提出结合数据流聚合与工作流分解的自动 harness 生成方法。该方法通过分析程序数据流并分解复杂工作流，生成更有效的测试入口。实验可能表明其能提高覆盖率并发现更多漏洞。

ACOFuzz: Memory-Error-Sensitive Fuzzing Driven by Address-Computation-Operand-Aware Feedback Mechanism

Yiru Zhao (Wuhan University), Aorui Zhang (Wuhan University), Xiaoke Wang (Wuhan University), Aoshuang Ye (Wuhan Textile University), Benxiao Tang (Wuhan Textile University), Jinxin Ma (School of Cyberspace Security, Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications), Lei Zhao (Wuhan University), Heqing Huang (City University of Hong Kong)

核心总结

该论文研究如何提升模糊测试对内存错误的检测能力。现有覆盖率引导的模糊器难以区分与内存错误密切相关的地址计算操作，导致反馈信号不够敏感。作者提出 ACOFuzz，引入地址计算操作数感知的反馈机制，优先探索可能触发非法内存访问的输入。实验表明该方法能更高效地发现内存错误，提升漏洞挖掘效果。

Compiler Provenance as a Service: Decoupled Identification for Composite Provenance and Operational Resilience

Han Gao (Purdue University), Antonio Bianchi (Purdue University), Z. Berkay Celik (Purdue University), Dave (Jing) Tian (Purdue University)

核心总结

该论文针对编译器溯源问题，提出了一种名为“编译器溯源即服务”的解耦识别方法。传统方法在复合溯源和操作弹性方面存在局限，难以应对复杂的软件供应链攻击。作者设计了一种服务架构，将溯源标识与二进制文件解耦，从而支持复合溯源并提升操作弹性。实验表明该方法在准确性和鲁棒性上优于现有方案。

PyFEX: Uncovering Evasive Python-based Threats via Resilient and Exhaustive Path Exploration

Meng Wang (CISPA Helmholtz Center for Information Security), Yue Ma (University of Electronic Science and Technology), Majid Garoosi (CISPA Helmholtz Center for Information Security), Wenting Fan (Shanghai Jiao Tong University), Liwei Guo (University of Electronic Science and Technology), Jiangqiang Wang (Max Planck Institute for Security and Privacy), Ali Abbasi (CISPA Helmholtz Center for Information Security)

针对日益增长的Python恶意软件威胁，该论文提出了PyFEX框架。传统方法难以应对具有逃避性的Python威胁，PyFEX通过弹性且穷尽的路径探索技术，有效揭示恶意行为。实验表明PyFEX能够发现现有工具遗漏的威胁，提高了检测覆盖率。

Hieronym: Leveraging Hierarchical Multi-Source Information for Function Renaming in Stripped Binary

Xiaoling Zhang (Zhongguancun Laboratory), Jian Sun (Zhongguancun Laboratory), Dawei Wang (Zhongguancun Laboratory), Chongyu Wang (Zhongguancun Laboratory), Li Chen (Zhongguancun Laboratory), zhaoteng yan (Zhongguancun Laboratory), peipei liu (Zhongguancun Laboratory), Lixiao Zhang (Harbin Institute of Technology), Dan Li (Tsinghua University)

在剥离符号的二进制中恢复函数名对逆向工程至关重要，但现有方法依赖单一信息源，准确率有限。本文提出 Hieronym，利用分层多源信息（如调用图、字符串、常量等）进行函数重命名，通过图神经网络融合多层次特征。在多个真实二进制数据集上，Hieronym 的命名准确率显著优于现有工具，尤其在复杂函数上表现突出。

Forced Execution for Malware Protected by Commercial Virtualization Obfuscators

Yifei Zhan (Renmin University of China), Yukun Cui (Renmin University of China), Shuofeng Hao (Renmin University of China), Dongnan He (Renmin University of China), Wei You (Renmin University of China), Bin Liang (Renmin University of China), Jianjun Huang (Renmin University of China)

商业虚拟化混淆器通过将恶意代码转换为自定义字节码来对抗分析，给恶意软件逆向带来挑战。本文提出一种强制执行技术，通过监控虚拟化解释器的执行并记录语义等价的原始指令，绕过混淆。实验表明，该方法能有效解混淆多个商业混淆器保护的恶意软件样本，恢复出可读的原始代码，且性能开销可接受。

Cong Zhang (Institute for Advanced Study, Tsinghua University), Yang Cao (School of Cyber Science and Technology, Shandong University), Yujie Bai (Hangzhou High-Tech Zone (Binjiang) Institute of Blockchain and Data Security), Shuaishuai Li (Zhongguancun Laboratory, Beijing, China), Juntong Lin (Shandong University), Yu Chen (Shandong University), Anyu Wang (Tsinghua University), Xiaoyun Wang (Tsinghua University)

核心总结

模糊私有集合交集（Fuzzy PSI）允许各方在保护隐私的前提下判断集合元素是否近似匹配，但高维数据会带来维度灾难。论文针对现有方案中O((log δ)^d)开销问题，提出新的密码学构造以避免这一指数级依赖。方法结合高效编码、哈希或不经意传输等技术实现模糊匹配。安全分析与性能评估表明，新协议在保持隐私性的同时显著降低通信和计算开销，适用于高维模糊PSI场景。

Time-Delayed Publicly Verifiable Quantum Computation with Classical Verifiers

Ameer Mohammed (Kuwait University), Aydin Abadi (Newcastle University), Jaffer Mahdi (Kuwait University)

核心总结

可验证量子计算旨在让有限能力的客户端验证云量子服务器计算结果的正确性，但公开可验证且具有时间延迟特性的方案仍具挑战。论文提出一种带时间延迟、可公开验证的量子计算协议，并支持经典验证者参与验证。构造结合时间锁谜题、可验证盲量子计算或密码承诺等技术。安全性分析表明协议满足公开可验证性和时间延迟性质，且经典客户端无需量子能力即可完成验证。

Resilient Alerting Protocols for Blockchains

Marwa Mouallem (Technion), Lorenz Breidenbach (Chainlink Labs), Ittay Eyal (Technion), Ari Juels (Chainlink Labs, Cornell Tech, IC3)

核心总结

区块链应用需要及时告警异常交易或攻击，但现有告警协议可能被操纵或不可靠。论文研究面向区块链的弹性告警协议，设计机制以保证告警在对抗环境下的正确性与可用性。方法结合经济激励、密码学承诺或共识机制来抵御恶意干扰。分析表明协议能提升区块链安全监控的鲁棒性，为去中心化告警提供新思路。

Walrus: An Efficient Decentralized Storage Network

George Danezis (Mysten Labs & University College London (UCL)), Giacomo Giuliari (Mysten Labs), Lefteris Kokoris-Kogias (MystenLabs), Markus Legner (Mysten Labs), Jean-Pierre Smith (Mysten Labs), Alberto Sonnino (Mysten Labs & University College London (UCL)), Karl Wüst (Mysten Labs)

核心总结

去中心化存储网络在可扩展性、数据可用性和完整性验证方面面临挑战。Walrus 提出一种高效的去中心化存储网络，可能结合纠删码、认证数据结构和激励/共识机制，在保证安全性的同时提升存储与读取效率。该工作通过系统设计与评估展示其在去中心化存储场景中的性能优势。

NFSA: Non-Forward Secure Aggregation with One Server via Two Layer Secret Sharing

Yufei Zhou (Sun Yat-sen University)

核心总结

安全聚合允许服务器聚合客户端更新而不查看个体数据，但现有方案常依赖多服务器或受前向安全要求限制。NFSA 提出基于两层秘密共享的单服务器非前向安全聚合方案。该方法旨在降低通信与计算开销，同时保持聚合过程的隐私性。安全分析和实验评估验证了其可行性与实用性。

A Formal Analysis of Signal’s Sparse Post-Quantum Ratchet Protocol and its Implementation

Karthikeyan Bhargavan (Cryspen), Maxime Buyse (Cryspen), Graeme Connell (Signal), Franziskus Kiefer (Cryspen), Rolfe Schmidt (Signal)

核心总结

Signal 协议引入稀疏后量子棘轮以增强对量子攻击的抵抗能力，但其形式化安全性与实现正确性仍需严格验证。论文对该后量子棘轮协议及其实现进行形式化分析，建立安全模型并检查协议与代码实现的一致性。该工作旨在确认或发现潜在安全问题，为后量子安全通信协议的设计与部署提供依据。

Designated-Verifier Dynamic zk-SNARKs with Applications to Dynamic Proofs of Index

Weijie Wang (Yale University), Charalampos Papamanthou (Yale University and Lagrange Labs), Shravan Srinivasan (Lagrange Labs), Dimitrios Papadopoulos (Hong Kong University of Science & Technology)

核心总结

动态 zk-SNARK 允许在数据更新后高效更新证明，但在指定验证者场景下如何兼顾动态性与隐私仍具挑战。该论文提出指定验证者动态 zk-SNARK 方案，支持证明的动态更新，并仅允许指定验证者验证证明内容。作者将该方案应用于动态索引证明（dynamic proofs of index），构造了相应的协议。分析表明，该方案在保证隐私和动态性的同时具有较好的效率。

BABE: Verifying Proofs on Bitcoin Made 1000x Cheaper

Sanjam Garg (University of California, Berkeley and Exponential Science Foundation), Dimitris Kolonelos (UC Berkeley), Mikhail Sergeevitch (Babylon Labs), Srivatsan Sridhar (Byzantine Research), David Tse (Byzantine Research and Stanford University)

核心总结

在比特币上验证 SNARK 证明通常需要高昂的脚本执行和交易费用，限制了其实际应用。该论文提出 BABE 协议，通过巧妙的密码学构造和比特币脚本优化，大幅降低链上证明验证成本。作者实现了该方案并在比特币网络上进行了评估，结果显示验证成本降低了约 1000 倍。这一工作使比特币能够以更实际的方式支持证明验证，扩展了其应用场景。

On the Limits of Consensus under Dynamic Availability and Reconfiguration

Javier Nieto (University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign), Joachim Neu (a16z Crypto Research), Ling Ren (University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign)

核心总结

共识协议在节点动态加入离开和重新配置时，其安全性和活性面临理论极限。该论文研究动态可用性和重新配置条件下的共识不可能性，形式化定义了相关模型和攻击场景。作者证明了在满足某些动态可用性和重新配置需求时，共识协议无法同时保证所有期望属性。研究结果揭示了现有区块链共识假设的局限性，并为设计更鲁棒的协议提供了理论指导。

Angelfish: Leader, DAG, or Anywhere in Between

Qianyu Yu (HKUST(GZ)), Giuliano Losa (Stellar Development Foundation), Nibesh Shrestha (Supra Research), Xuechao Wang (HKUST(GZ))

核心总结

拜占庭容错共识协议在性能与抗审查性之间存在权衡：领导者协议吞吐高但易受审查，DAG 协议抗审查但开销大。本文提出 Angelfish，一个可在领导者与 DAG 之间连续调节的共识框架，允许根据部署需求选择中间形态。该工作形式化分析了不同配置下的吞吐、延迟与抗审查性关系。实验表明，Angelfish 能在保持高吞吐的同时提供可配置的审查抵抗能力。

Censorship Resistance vs Throughput in Multi-Proposer BFT Protocols

Fatima Elsheimy (Yale University), Ioannis Kaklamanis (Yale University, IC3), Charalampos Papamanthou (Yale University), Sarisht Wadhwa (Duke University), Fan Zhang (Yale University, IC3)

核心总结

多提议者拜占庭容错协议旨在提升吞吐量并降低单点审查风险，但抗审查性与吞吐量之间的内在权衡尚缺乏系统分析。本文对多提议者 BFT 协议进行形式化建模，量化不同提议者数量、轮次安排与网络条件下的审查抵抗和吞吐表现。研究揭示了提高吞吐可能削弱抗审查性的边界条件，并给出了协议设计建议。实验与分析结果为构建兼顾性能与公平性的共识系统提供了依据。

Private Signaling Secure Against Actively Corrupted Servers

Haotian Chu (Northwestern University), Xiao Wang (Northwestern University), Yanxue Jia (Illinois Institute of Technology)

核心总结

私密信令允许发送者在不向服务器泄露接收者或消息内容的情况下完成通知，现有方案多假设服务器半诚实。本文研究服务器主动腐化场景下的私密信令安全，提出可抵抗主动攻击的协议构造。方案可能结合多方计算或密码学承诺等机制，保证恶意服务器无法破坏隐私或正确性。论文给出形式化安全证明与性能评估。

Decoupled TFHE-type Functional Bootstrapping

Zhihao Li (Ant Digital Technologies, Ant Group), Xuan Shen (Key Laboratory of Cyberspace Security Defense, Institute of Information Engineering, CAS; School of Cyber Security, UCAS), Xianhui Lu (Key Laboratory of Cyberspace Security Defense, Institute of Information Engineering, CAS; School of Cyber Security, UCAS), Ruida Wang (Key Laboratory of Cyberspace Security Defense, Institute of Information Engineering, CAS; School of Cyber Security, UCAS), Yuan Zhao (Ant Digital Technologies, Ant Group), Zhiwei Wang (Key Laboratory of Cyberspace Security Defense, Institute of Information Engineering, CAS; School of Cyber Security, UCAS)

核心总结

TFHE 的功能自举可在自举过程中同时计算函数，是全同态加密中的关键但昂贵操作。本文提出解耦式 TFHE 型功能自举，将函数计算与自举过程分离，以降低复杂度或支持更灵活的函数求值。通过理论分析与实验评估，该方法在保持安全性的同时提升了功能自举效率。

Compiling Oblivious Schemes Against Coercion: The Anamorphic Construction

Xianglong Zhang (Huazhong University of Science and Technology), Wei Wang (Huazhong University of Science and Technology), Peng Xu (Huazhong University of Science and Technology), Laurence T. Yang (St. Francis Xavier University), Stjepan Picek (Radboud University and University of Zagreb), Kaitai Liang (University of Turku and TU Delft)

核心总结

抗胁迫加密旨在使用户在被迫交出密钥时仍能隐藏真实信息。本文研究如何将不经意（oblivious）方案编译为抗胁迫方案，提出一种 anamorphic 构造。该方法可能通过隐蔽密钥或额外信道实现，使得胁迫者无法区分正常与隐藏消息。论文给出安全定义、构造与证明。

DeepProve: Verifiable End-to-End Large Language Model Inference

Nicolas Gailly (Lagrange Labs), Ismael Hishon-Rezaizadeh (Lagrange Labs), Tianyi Liu (University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign and Lagrange Labs), Nicholas Mainardi (Lagrange Labs), Dimitrios Papadopoulos (Hong Kong University of Science & Technology), Charalampos Papamanthou (Yale University and Lagrange Labs), Christodoulos Pappas (Hong Kong University of Science & Technology and Lagrange Labs), Shravan Srinivasan (Lagrange Labs), Zack Youell (Lagrange Labs), Yupeng Zhang (University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign and Lagrange Labs)

核心总结

大语言模型推理常外包给不可信服务商，用户需要验证输出是否由指定模型正确计算。本文提出 DeepProve，实现端到端可验证的 LLM 推理。方法可能利用零知识证明或可验证计算，将模型推理过程转化为可验证电路。实验评估了证明生成与验证开销，展示其可扩展性。

Melior, Verifiable Mixed-Integer Programming via zkSNARKs

Wenjie Qu (National University of Singapore), Yanze Jiang (National University of Singapore), Yanpei Guo (National University of Singapore), Jiaheng Zhang (National University of Singapore)

核心总结

混合整数规划（MIP）广泛用于调度、资源分配等安全敏感场景，但当求解过程外包给不可信服务器时，用户难以验证返回解的最优性与可行性。Melior 提出基于 zkSNARK 的可验证混合整数规划框架，将 MIP 求解结果与最优性证明绑定，使验证者无需访问私有目标函数和约束即可完成验证。该工作针对整数变量和线性约束设计高效证明电路，降低了证明生成与验证开销。实验表明其在典型 MIP 规模下具有可接受的证明开销，为可验证优化外包提供了新思路。

Braid: Sybil-Resistant Decentralized Identity with Trustless Key Recovery and Non-Transferable Anonymous Credentials

SONG Rui (Department of Computing, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University), ZHENG Tianyu (Department of Computing, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University), Shang Gao (the hong kong polytechnic university), Guyue Li (School of Cyber Science and Engineering, Southeast University), Xue Liu (McGill University), Bin Xiao (The Hong Kong Polytechnic Univ.)

核心总结
